🏒 Special Co-Founder Board Evaluation (Seats 1, 2, 3 & 4 Candid Consensus) Ranks #1, #2, #4 & #5 Below
Question: How should plays start on the ice across 8U–18U and Rec–AAA — when should skaters/puck animate in motion for 2 seconds before freezing at the decision read vs. when is a static snapshot better for learning?
1. 8U & 10U (Rec / B) — Calm Static Default

Start: Calm Static Setup + 1.0s Pulsing Gold "YOU #9" Ring + Optional [▶️ Watch 2s Lead-In] button.
Why (Seats 2 & 3): Automatic motion on load causes 6–10 year olds to "puck-watch" the moving dot instead of scanning the Attack Triangle or The House.

2. 10U (A–AAA) & 12U (Rec / B) — Guided Slow-Roll

Start: 1.5s Guided Slow-Roll Lead-In (0.65x speed) → Auto-Freeze at Decision Point + [🔄 Replay Lead-In].
Why (Seat 2): Bridges static chalkboards to live hockey by showing direction of travel without overwhelming working memory.

3. 12U (A–AAA) & 14U–18U — 2.0s Live Roll-In

Start: 2.0s Full-Speed Pre-Snap Motion Roll-In → Crisp "Skate-Stop Freeze" at the decision read.
Why (Seats 1 & 4): At travel/HS/AAA speeds, closing speed and gap momentum ARE the read. A dead static snapshot hides whether D1's gap is tight or loose.
